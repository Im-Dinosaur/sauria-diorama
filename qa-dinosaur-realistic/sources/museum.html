Guide for grown-ups

When it lived
Late Jurassic
Where fossils were found
western North America
Body length
6.5–9 m (about 21–30 ft)
Diet
Plant-eater
What kind of animal?
Stegosaur dinosaur. Stegosaurus belonged to the stegosaurs, a branch of the ornithischian dinosaurs. The bony plates along its back and the spikes at the end of its tail are its easiest features to recognise.

Research notes about Stegosaurus

Research summary

Fossils of Stegosaurus date to the Late Jurassic and have been found in western North America.

Learning from fossils

  • The length is an approximate range suitable for family reading; estimates from different specimens are not presented as one exact measurement.

About this museum

A little museum made by a developer dad for his daughter

I’m Leon, a developer and a dad. When my daughter was three, dinosaur chases on television frightened her, so I made a 3D prehistoric animal museum where she could look quietly and listen only when she wanted to.

The museum is free, with no sign-up, adverts or visitor tracking. Finding one interesting detail at a time is plenty.
